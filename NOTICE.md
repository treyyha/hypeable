## Design reference

`design-reference/` (gitignored) may hold a third-party dashboard template used as a **visual and structural** reference only. Its tree had no LICENSE file. No template source was copied into Hypeable; the shell, tokens, and components were re-implemented on Remix + UnoCSS.
